### 1. PGDM (flagship)

- **What it is:** WeSchool's two-year, full-time flagship PGDM combining general management with a functional specialization in the second year. [Mumbai page](https://www.welingkar.org/pgdm/pgdm_mumbai.php)
- **Specializations:** Marketing, HR, Finance, Operations. The Bengaluru page overview also mentions Analytics; CMC must confirm before the agent mentions it. [Bengaluru page](https://welingkar.org/pgdm/pgdm_bengaluru.php)
- **Roles the site lists:** Equity Analyst, Business Analyst, Product Manager, HR Generalist, Supply Chain Manager.
- **Distinctive features (Bengaluru page):** an assessment centre, a development centre with Excel training, domain and aptitude tests, mock GDs and PIs, a "Finance GYM" equity game, and industry and alumni roundtables. [PGDM Core, Bengaluru](https://www.welingkar.org/pgdm-bengaluru/PGDM-Core.html)
- **Pitch line:** "Our flagship batch gives you functional specialists in Marketing, Finance, HR and Operations."

### 2. PGDM E-Business

- **What it is:** a program that combines business and technology, building digital proficiency for business problems. [E-Business Mumbai page](https://welingkar.org/pgdm/pgdm_e-biz_mumbai.php)
- **Specializations (Trimesters V–VI) and sample courses** [E-Business brochure 2026–28](https://www.welingkar.org/downloads/PGDM_E_business_brochure_final_2026.pdf):
    - Marketing: Strategic Marketing, Digital Marketing, Advertising and Brand Management, Marketing Metrics, Performance Marketing
    - Finance: Advanced Financial Management, Essentials of Banking, FinTech, Financial Risk Management, Security Analysis and Portfolio Management
    - Operations: Materials Management, Production Planning and Control, Logistics & Supply Chain Management, Design and New Product Development
    - Human Resources: HR Analytics, Compensation Management, Employee Relations, Organizational Development and Change Management
- **Industry verticals (choose one):** Consulting, Advanced Business Analytics, World Class Manufacturing, Indian FMCG Industry, International Business, IT Project Management, Governance Risk and Compliance, Digital Business Strategy, Supply Chain Analytics, Indian Consumer Durable Industry.
- **Technology courses:** e-Business Foundations, Emerging Technologies, Enterprise Business Applications, AI in Business, Business Process Management and RPA, Design Thinking and Innovation, Information Control and Security.
- **Roles the brochure lists:** Business Analyst, Management Consultant, Functional Consultant, Data Analyst, Product Owner, Digital Product Manager, Presales Specialist, UX/Customer Experience Manager, Digital Marketing Specialist.
- **Pitch line:** "E-Business students take a full functional specialization plus technology courses like AI in Business and RPA — useful for digital, product and consulting roles."

### 3. PGDM Research & Business Analytics (RBA)

- **What it is:** a PGDM with a functional specialization plus a deep analytics track. [RBA brochure 2025–27](https://www.welingkar.org/downloads/PGDM_Research_%26_Business_Analytics_July%202025.pdf)
- **Specializations:** Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations.
- **Analytics courses:** Python, SQL, Excel with Macros, Data Mining (supervised and unsupervised), Data Warehousing and BI, Visual Analytics and BI Reporting, Predictive Analytics, Text Analytics, Fraud Detection and Risk Analytics, HR Analytics, Analytics for Marketing, Algorithms in Machine Learning, Big Data, Deployment of ML Models, Deep Learning, Capstone Project.
- **Tools named on the website:** Python, R, Tableau, IBM-SPSS, Frontline Analytic Solver, Palisade Decision Tool. [RBA Mumbai page](https://welingkar.org/pgdm/pgdm_research_bussiness_analytics_mumbai.php)
- **Certifications via Bloomberg Lab:** Data Visualization and Data Mining; Portfolio Management and ESG Analytics; Bloomberg Market Concepts.
- **Roles the brochure lists:** Management Consultant, Data Analyst/Scientist, Business Analyst, Research Analyst, BI Consultant, Pricing and Revenue Optimization Analyst, Financial Analyst, Supply Chain Analyst, Product Analyst, HR Analyst, Project Manager.
- **Pitch line:** "RBA students carry a functional specialization plus hands-on Python, SQL and machine learning."

**Routing guide for the agent:**

- Recruiter wants **HR / HRBP / talent** profiles → HR specialists from PGDM, E-Business, RBA, Retail; HR Analytics is taught in E-Business and RBA; Healthcare lists Healthcare Talent Acquisition roles.
- Wants **finance / BFSI / analytics-heavy finance** → Finance specialists from PGDM, E-Business, RBA, Retail; RBA adds Bloomberg Lab certifications; Rural covers microfinance and priority sector lending.
- Wants **sales / brand / marketing** → Marketing specialists from PGDM, E-Business, RBA, Retail; Media & Entertainment for media sales and brand roles; Rural for rural sales; Healthcare (Pharma track) for pharma sales and brand roles.
- Wants **operations / supply chain** → Operations specialists from PGDM, E-Business, RBA, Retail; Healthcare for hospital operations.
- Wants **product / digital / consulting** → E-Business, Business Design, RBA.
- Anything else → current programme/profile fit needs CMC review; capture the question in the transcript without promising an email or notifying a human.

## Internships and ways recruiters can engage

The brochures confirm a Summer Internship Project (SIP) with a Summer Internship Research Project (SIRP) in Trimester IV, plus live projects and industry immersions; they do not publish a placement calendar or internship start dates. The agent can describe these engagement formats but must not commit to dates.

| Engagement format | Verified detail | Programs where stated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer internship (SIP + SIRP) | Listed in Trimester IV | Retail, Media & Entertainment, RBA, Business Design, E-Business (SIP) |
| Rural summer internship | 60 days with companies in the rural sector, inside a 4-month embedded rural internship | Rural Management |
| Live projects | "Live industry projects"; Healthcare lists live projects from Trimester 1 | Retail, Media & Entertainment, RBA, Healthcare |
| Industry immersion / dissertation | Healthcare: Industry Immersion Project – Master Dissertation in Trimester 6; Media: Industry Research Project in Trimester 6 | Healthcare, Media & Entertainment |
| International internship | Listed in the student life cycle; Business Design names an internship opportunity at Mattel, Mexico | RBA, E-Business ("Global internship programs"), Business Design |
| Guest sessions, roundtables, field visits | Industry and alumni roundtables, guest sessions, field and immersion visits | PGDM (Bengaluru), Healthcare, Media & Entertainment, Retail |

**What the agent can offer a recruiter (without committing dates):**

1. Summer internship hiring (SIP).
2. Final placement hiring for full-time roles.
3. A live project or case problem for students.
4. A guest session or industry roundtable.
5. A program-specific route: a rural internship (Rural Management) or healthcare industry immersion (Healthcare).

**What it must not say:** internship start dates, internship length (except Rural's stated 60 days), stipend expectations, the placement season calendar, or the number of students available. These go on the "Not verified" list for CMC.
